# Senior Customer Service Representative Interview Notes

## Does the current problem statement align with your understanding?

- The biggest issue is consistency.
- Complaints are not handled consistently within Customer Service despite existing guidance and training.
- Some aspects of the process are left up to the representative to make a decision.
- Representatives don't know the definition of a complaint that Compliance uses, and there isn't much visibility into how complaints are handled after being sent to Compliance.
- Agrees that reporting requires manual work. Representatives are sometimes pulled in to help clarify older complaint records during the reporting process.

## Can you walk me through what you actually do when a customer complains?

- The first priority is trying to resolve the customer's issue.
- If the issue is recognized as a complaint, a record is created with the category, description, customer information, and other relevant information.
- If the representative is unable to resolve the issue, they send it to the appropriate team or escalate it depending on the situation.
- Once the issue has been addressed, the complaint is marked as resolved.

## How do you decide whether something should be recorded as a complaint?

- The criteria for determining whether something should be recorded as a complaint are not completely clear.
- A vague negative comment from a customer would not necessarily be considered a complaint.
- Representatives consider the severity of the issue and whether it requires action.
- Sometimes a representative resolves a complaint immediately and doesn't think to create a complaint record afterward. The representative specifically mentioned that it is easy to move directly to the next customer interaction after resolving an issue.
- Different representatives may make these decisions differently.

## How do you choose a complaint category?

- Categorization is usually straightforward.
- The system only allows one primary category, so representatives have to make a judgment when a customer complains about more than one issue.
- If none of the categories seem appropriate, representatives use "Other."
- Representatives may interpret the existing categories differently.

## Are there places where the current process is difficult, unclear, or requires workarounds?

- Categorization is one of the biggest issues.
- Escalations can be confusing because it isn't always clear which team should receive an issue.
- Notes contain varying levels of detail depending on the representative.
- Complaints received from branches don't always contain enough information, requiring Customer Service to essentially restart the complaint process.
- The process is unclear when a customer contacts the bank multiple times about the same issue. It isn't always obvious whether the representative should create a new complaint record or update the existing one.

## Are there any data-quality issues you've noticed in complaint records?

- Inconsistent categories and notes.
- Duplicate records can occur when customers contact the bank through multiple channels.
- Branch information is not always complete.
- Product information can be difficult to capture when a complaint involves multiple accounts or products.
- Some complaints are not logged because the representative resolved the issue immediately and forgot to create a complaint record.

## If the complaint process or reporting were changed, what would you want the people designing it to understand about your day-to-day work?

- Do not introduce an additional system that representatives need to update.
- Representatives are already juggling multiple tasks while trying to help the customer.
- If additional information needs to be captured, make it clear why that information is needed.
- Providing better clarity around the existing categories would be preferable to adding more categories.
- It would be helpful if the system could automatically populate information it already knows rather than requiring representatives to enter it again.

## What would success look like from your perspective?

- Greater clarity around the complaint process.
- Ability to record complaints without interfering with the representative's ability to help the customer.
- Better visibility into what happens after complaints are escalated and what is being done to address them.
- Reduce the need for managers to come back to representatives for clarification while preparing reports.

## Is there anything else you think I should know or investigate?

- Talk to representatives who handle different types of customer contacts because their experiences may differ.
- Investigate how repeat contacts from the same customer about the same issue are currently handled.
- Look into what happens after a complaint has been escalated and whether more visibility into that process is needed.
