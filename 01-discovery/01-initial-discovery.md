# Initial Discovery

## Initial Request

The VP of Customer Experience requested a Power BI dashboard that would provide leadership with a reliable view of customer complaints. Existing complaint reporting is produced by multiple departments, and the reported numbers do not consistently align.

## Discovery Approach

The initial request proposed a Power BI dashboard as the solution. Before defining requirements or beginning development, I planned an initial discovery conversation with the VP of Customer Experience to better understand the underlying business problem and desired outcomes.

The questions were intentionally focused on the business context rather than technical or solution requirements. The goal was to determine why the initiative was needed, understand the current state at a high level, identify the appropriate stakeholders for deeper discovery, and establish how success should ultimately be evaluated.

Detailed process, data, and technical discovery would follow with the stakeholders closest to those areas.

## Prepared Discovery Questions

1. **What prompted this request?**
   Are you seeing evidence that customer complaints are increasing or changing, or is the primary concern that we don't currently have a reliable way to understand and act on customer feedback?

2. **What information do you have today?**
   Can you walk me through the reports or other sources you're currently using to understand customer complaints and, at a high level, how that information gets captured and reported today? What information does each provide, and where are you seeing inconsistencies?

3. **How is this information used today?**
   When leadership receives complaint information, what happens next? What kinds of decisions or actions does it currently inform?

4. **What do you need to understand about customer complaints that you can't reliably understand today?**
   What are the most important questions you want the eventual solution to answer?

5. **Who needs this information, and how might their needs differ?**
   Who uses complaint information today? Who do you expect would use a future solution, and what decisions would they need it to support?

6. **What would success look like?**
   If we can consistently answer the questions we've identified, is that sufficient for this initiative to be successful? Beyond answering those questions, are there other outcomes that would make this initiative successful?

7. **Who should be involved in discovery?**
   Which leaders, teams, subject-matter experts, and people performing the day-to-day work should I speak with? Who owns the relevant processes or data, and who could be impacted by changes?

8. **What should I know before moving forward?**
   Are there known constraints, risks, dependencies, previous attempts, regulatory considerations, deadlines, or other concerns that are top-of-mind?
